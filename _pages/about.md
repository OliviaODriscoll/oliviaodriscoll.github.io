I am a PhD student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at MIT. 

I am broadly interested in computer vision for image-guided surgical interventions. 

Before starting my PhD, I completed a B.S. (Magna Cum Laude) in Computer Science at Columbia University. At Columbia, I worked with Professor Andrew Laine in the Heffner Biomedical Imaging Lab, harmonizing quantitative imaging biomarkers form large cohorts of lung CT scans. I also worked in the Morfeus Lab at MD Anderson Cancer Center with Professors Kristy Brock and Tien Tang, developing predictive models for liver functional decline post-radiation therapy. Before Columbia, I worked with Professor Gabor Fichtinger in the Queen's University Laboratory for Percutaneous Surgery, developing computer vision methods for medical trainee skill assessment in central venous catheterization.
